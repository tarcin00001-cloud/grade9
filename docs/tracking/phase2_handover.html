What's actually wrong

These aren't stubs. Every one of the eleven already has a working LabShell with a real title, a real theme, and — unexpectedly — an “L##” lesson number baked into its own subtitle prop (L33 through L46). That's the signature of a planned extension sequence that got built and then never reconciled with the main catalog. The job here is registration, not construction.
